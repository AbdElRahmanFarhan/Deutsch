# Syllabus: Menschen A1 → A2

Primary source: **Menschen** (Hueber), Kursbuch + Arbeitsbuch, volumes A1.1, A1.2, A2.1, A2.2.
Local copies live in `Cirriculum/` (laptop only, never committed or uploaded).

Each Menschen Lektion is 4 Kursbuch pages: an opener with the lesson goals, a double page
introducing the new grammar and phrases, and a final page with speaking/writing training and a
summary of the grammar. Every 3 Lektionen there is a "Modul-Plus" section (Lesemagazin,
Film-Stationen, Projekt Landeskunde, Ausklang).

Page numbers below are the printed Kursbuch page where the Lektion starts.

## Weekly schedule

Session days, times and lengths are set in `config/schedule.toml`; phase dates and pace in
`config/plan.toml`. The default is 14 hours a week (Mon–Thu 1 h, Fri 3 h, Sat 2 × 2 h, Sun 3 h).

With that default, a normal A2 week covers 4 Lektionen:

| Day | Work |
|---|---|
| Mon + Tue | Lektion A: grammar + vocab (Mon), book exercises + writing (Tue) |
| Wed + Thu | Lektion B, same split |
| Fri | Lektion C in one go, plus Arbeitsbuch and listening |
| Sat block 1 | Lektion D |
| Sat block 2 | practice: mixed exercises, exam-style task, speaking session |
| Sun | Modul review (Modul-Plus pages), long speaking session, weekly mini-test, check-in |

Every 1-hour session starts with ~10 minutes of review (due words + recent mistakes).

## How a lesson uses the book

- The lesson names the Lektion and the Kursbuch/Arbeitsbuch pages to work through, plus the audio tracks.
- The book's exercises are done from the book and checked with the Arbeitsbuch answer key (`AB-LSCH`).
- The tutor adds: explanations in English, everyday examples from life in Germany, extra exercises, SRS review, a speaking prompt.
- Vocabulary comes from the Lektion's word field, cross-checked against the Goethe A1/A2 word lists.
