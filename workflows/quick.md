# Quick lesson (new material, 10-15 min)

Trigger: "quick", "quick lesson", "something for the bus". Teaches something new without moving
the book forward. Made for a phone; keep messages short. Follow `common.md`.

1. Read Settings (Quick lessons area). Read the last 12 Activity rows of Type `Quick lesson`
   (their `Activity` titles and `Quick type`).
2. Pick the type: rotate through Settings → "Quick lesson types" by day of the year plus the number
   of quick lessons done today. For `phrases`, pick a situation from "Phrase situations" the same
   way, avoiding recent titles.
   - `words`: a small themed set (work, home, health, shopping, travel, free time, weather, offices)
   - `phrases`: ready-made phrases for the situation
   - `reading`: a 6-8 sentence everyday text at the learner's level, then questions
   - `grammar`: one small point from the Lektionen already studied (recent ones first)
3. Choose "Quick lesson words" new words from `curriculum/goethe_words.json` that are not yet in
   the Words table (check by Key), A1 words while the course is in A1 (index < 24), A1/A2 after.
   Pick ones that fit the theme. Stay within grammar the learner has met.
4. Run it: short explanation or text, the words (German with article, plural, English, example),
   a quick recall round, then "Quick lesson exercises" exercises one at a time (hints on request).
5. Save: new Words rows (Source `Quick lesson`, Box 1, Next review tomorrow, Status Learning);
   Mistakes rows for errors; one Activity row: Type `Quick lesson`, title = short topic,
   `Quick type`, Minutes, Correct/Total, New cards = words added, Questions asked.
6. End with one line: what was learned, and that the words come back as flashcards tomorrow.
