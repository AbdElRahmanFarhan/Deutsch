# Speaking practice

Trigger: "speak", "let's talk". Best in Claude app voice mode. Follow `common.md`.

1. Read Settings (Tutor area). Find the current Lektion (topic, grammar) and the speaking prompt
   of its latest lesson page, plus up to 12 words due today and the 5 most recent mistakes.
2. Tell the learner the topic in one line, then start. Speak slowly, simple A1-A2 German, one
   question at a time. Make them use the due words and the Lektion's grammar. Situations from
   everyday life in Germany.
3. Settings → "Correct during speaking": if no, don't correct while talking; note mistakes.
   If the learner says "Hilfe" or is stuck: the English meaning and a simpler way to say it.
4. When the learner says "Ende" or after about 15-20 minutes: list the biggest mistakes
   (Settings → "Speaking mistakes listed") with correction and reason, and one thing that went well.
5. Save: Mistakes rows (Source `speaking`, Category pronunciation where relevant); Words rows for
   any new words you taught (Source `Speaking`); one Activity row: Type `Speaking`, Minutes,
   Notes (one line). If a planned session of kind `practice` is today and still Planned, ask
   whether this counts as it; if yes, mark it Done.
